# CardKeep 0.6.2 Phone Test Plan

**Build under test:** CardKeep 0.6.2 (version code 12) from GitHub Releases, file `CardKeep-0.6.2-arm64-v8a.apk`.
**Covers:** every feature in the app (roadmap S0 to S14), every fix from the 0.6.1/0.6.2 audit, and the audit's
manual-review items that can only be checked on a real phone.
**Time:** the smoke pass takes about 15 minutes; the full pass takes 2 to 3 hours and can be split by section.

All names, numbers and addresses in this plan are fictional. Use your own real cards where a step says "a real card".

---

## How to use this plan

1. Do **Part 1, Setup**, once.
2. Do **Part 2, Smoke test** (15 minutes). If anything in it fails, stop and report it: the full pass will hit the
   same problem.
3. Work through **Part 3, Full pass**, section by section, in any order. Each test has numbered steps, the expected
   result, and a box to tick Pass or Fail with notes.
4. Fill in the **Summary checklist** below as you go, then send the report described in **How to report a failure**.

**Words used in this plan**

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Drawer | The side menu: tap the round button with two lines at the top left of Contacts. |
| + menu | The round **+** button at the top right of Contacts. |
| Force-stop | Android Settings > Apps > CardKeep > **Force stop**. Simulates the app being killed. |
| Review queue | Drawer > **Review queue**: scanned cards waiting for you to check and save. |
| Snackbar | The short message bar that appears at the bottom of the screen. |

## How to report a failure

For every Fail:

1. Take a screenshot (power + volume down) of the screen at the moment it went wrong.
2. Write down the **test ID** (for example `SCAN-04`), the **step number**, and what you saw instead of the expected
   result.
3. If the app closed by itself, say so, and say what you tapped just before.
4. Send: phone model, Android version (Settings > About phone), the list of failed IDs with notes, and the screenshots
   named by test ID (`SCAN-04.png`).

# Part 1: Setup

### What to have ready

- Your Android phone, charged above 50%, with at least 500 MB free.
- **5 to 10 real business cards**, including at least one two-sided card, one card with a fax number, one card with a
  name in ALL CAPS, one dark card with light text, and one card with a QR code if you have one.
- **A plain table** with a contrasting colour (cards scan best on a dark surface).
- **NFC (optional):** a tap card (Popl, Linq, Blinq, or similar), or a blank NFC sticker, or a second phone. A payment
  card or door badge for the "not read" check.
- **Sample files:** the `.vcf` and `.csv` in **Appendix A**. Copy them to the phone (email them to yourself, or save
  them to Downloads). A file manager app (Files by Google is fine).
- A computer or second device is **not** needed.

### SET-01: Download and install

**Checks:** the APK installs from GitHub.

1. On the phone, open `https://github.com/AxialForge/cardkeep/releases/latest` in Chrome.
2. Under **Assets**, tap `CardKeep-0.6.2-arm64-v8a.apk`. If Chrome warns that the file may be harmful, tap
   **Download anyway**.
3. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, tap **Settings** and turn on **Allow from this source** for Chrome, then
   go back.
4. Tap **Install** (or **Update** if an older CardKeep is installed).
5. Tap **Open**.

**Expected:** CardKeep opens on **Contacts**. If you updated from an older version, your contacts are still there.

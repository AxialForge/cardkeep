# Phone test sheet: CardKeep 0.2.0

**Install:** download `CardKeep-0.2.0-arm64-v8a.apk` from the GitHub Release and open it. It installs over 0.1.x and
keeps your contacts (Android says "Update"). Tick each line; note anything odd next to it. The emulator already
passed everything here with photos from the gallery; this sheet is about the real camera, real cards and real speed.

Have ready: 10 or more real business cards (include a dark one, a glossy one, a vertical one, a two-sided one, and
one with a QR code if you have it), a table, and decent light plus a dim spot.

## Updates (new)
- [ ] Settings → Updates → **Check for updates** says 0.2.0 is the latest.
- [ ] "Check when CardKeep opens" is off. Turn it on, close and reopen CardKeep: nothing appears (you're up to date).
  Turn it back off if you prefer.
- [ ] Later, when 0.2.1+ exists: the update installs from inside CardKeep. The first time, Android asks to allow
  installs from CardKeep. Your contacts are still there afterwards.

## Camera scanning: One card
- [ ] Scan card → the camera asks for permission once, then shows the live view.
- [ ] Point at a card on a table: an orange outline follows the card; the hint says "Move closer" / "Hold steady" /
  "Glare, tilt the card" / "More light" as appropriate.
- [ ] Hold still: it captures by itself (a short buzz). The shutter also works by hand.
- [ ] Torch button turns the light on and off. Tapping the view focuses there.
- [ ] The sheet shows the straightened card. **Use this card** opens Review scan with the fields filled in.
- [ ] Note how long capture → Review takes (goal: under about 2.5 s): ______
- [ ] **Add the back side**: photograph the back; its details fill gaps (e.g. an address on the back).
- [ ] **Adjust corners**: drag the four corners onto a card the outline missed; the card is straightened.
- [ ] **Retake** removes the card; closing the sheet keeps it in the Review queue.

## Review scan
- [ ] Each line of text on the card has a coloured box. Tap one, choose a field (e.g. Notes): it lands there.
- [ ] **Read a region**: drag a box over some small text; it's read and offered for a field.
- [ ] Fields the reader was unsure about are outlined amber with "Check this against the card".
- [ ] A card with a QR code: the details from the QR are used (check the company spelling is right).
- [ ] A card with a website QR: "This card has a web link" shows the real site name; it only opens when you tap.
- [ ] **Save** opens the new contact with its card image; **Save and review next** goes to the next waiting card.

## Batch and Pile
- [ ] Switch to **Batch**: scan 6–10 cards one after another without stopping. Each capture gives a short buzz;
  the orange chip shows "N reading · M to review".
- [ ] It doesn't capture the same card twice while it stays in view; it waits until you swap cards.
- [ ] Note roughly how many cards per minute you managed: ______ (goal: about 20)
- [ ] Review queue: "Saved automatically today" counts the clear ones (they're already in Contacts); the rest are
  listed with a reason (Blurry, Glare, Low confidence, No name found).
- [ ] Swipe a queued card left: it's removed; **Undo** brings it back.
- [ ] Switch to **Pile**: lay 3–4 cards on a table, one photo; each card appears in the queue.
- [ ] Settings → turn off "Save clear cards automatically": batch cards all wait in the queue instead.

## Safety
- [ ] Start a Batch of a few cards and swipe CardKeep away from recents while it's still reading. Reopen: it says
  "Reading N cards left from last time" and they appear in the queue.
- [ ] Turn the phone's dark mode on and off with the Scan and Review screens open; they stay readable.

## Report
Phone model and Android version, any unticked line, the two timings, and photos of any card it read badly (those
go into the test set: `test/cards`).

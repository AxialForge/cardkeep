# Phone test sheet: CardKeep 0.6.0 (tap cards)

**Install:** Settings → Updates → **Check for updates** offers 0.6.0. NFC can only be tested on a phone; the emulator
has none. Have ready: your 3 to 5 real tap cards (a contact card, a link card, any card whose link changes each tap),
and a payment card or door badge for the "not read" check.

## Tapping
- [ ] + → **Tap an NFC card** (or drawer → Tap cards): "Hold a card to the back of the phone".
- [ ] Turn NFC off in Android settings, open Tap cards: "NFC is off" → **Open NFC settings** goes there; turn it on
  and come back: it starts listening by itself.
- [ ] Tap a **contact** card: a buzz, and a row with the name. **Review and save** opens the form filled in; Save.
- [ ] Tap the **same card** again straight away: no second row. Wait a few seconds and tap again: it reads again.
- [ ] Tap a **link** card: **Open** shows the page in your browser; **Save link only** adds a contact with that
  website and the "NFC link" tag.
- [ ] A card whose link changes every tap: tapping twice quickly still counts once.
- [ ] Tap several cards one after another without leaving the screen; each gets its own row.
- [ ] A payment card or door badge: "Not read" (nothing from it is shown or kept).
- [ ] Note where the reader is on your phone (middle of the back, near the camera…): ______

## With events and duplicates
- [ ] Start an event's scan session, then tap a contact card and save it: the contact shows **Met at** that event.
- [ ] Tap the card of someone you already have: after saving, their page shows a possible duplicate (or, at a new
  company, a possible job change).

## Report
Phone model, any unticked line, and which cards didn't read (brand of the card if you know it).

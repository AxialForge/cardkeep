# Phone test sheet: CardKeep 0.4.0

**Install:** Settings → Updates → **Check for updates** offers 0.4.0. Your contacts stay. Lines from the 0.3.0 sheet
that you haven't ticked yet still apply.

## Career tracking (new)
- [ ] Rescan the card of someone you have, after they changed company (or edit a copy to another company and scan
  it): the old contact's page shows **A new card shows them at <company>**, and it's listed in drawer → **Career
  moves**.
- [ ] **Review** → **Add as new role, keep history**: their page shows the new company; the old job is listed as
  "Previously"; the old work email is struck through (kept as history) and Email uses the new one.
- [ ] Try **Replace old details** on another: the old job and work email are gone.
- [ ] **Different person**: nothing changes, and the pair isn't suggested again.
- [ ] On a contact: ⋯ → **Changed jobs…**, type the new company, pick who took over: the old page shows **Replaced
  as <title> by <name>** with Call and Email; the replacement's page shows **Replaces**.
- [ ] With a follow-up set, tick **Move the follow-up** when choosing the replacement: it moves to them.
- [ ] ⋯ → **Mark retired…** (pick a replacement or close the picker to skip): Retired shows on the page and under
  the Retired filter; ⋯ → **Not retired** undoes it.
- [ ] Long-press an email → **Mark as bounced**: it's struck through, no longer used by Email, and the person shows
  up in Career moves.
- [ ] Settings → **Career tracking** off: new scans and bounces no longer create suggestions.

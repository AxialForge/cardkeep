1 · Capture

Three ways in. Use whichever the card allows.

Photo

Single, batch, or up to four cards in one frame. On-device OCR reads the text.

QR code

A vCard or MECARD code is read directly. More accurate than OCR.

NFC tap

Tap a tap-card. Most hold a link; some hold a full contact.

Open the Scan screen, pick a mode, and point or tap. The app handles the rest.

Order of preference: QR, then NFC vCard, then photo. Photo is the universal fallback.

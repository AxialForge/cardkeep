NFC tap cards

Tap the card. Choose what to do with it.

Link card

The most common kind. Shows the domain, then opens in your default browser.

Contact card

Holds a full vCard. One tap on Add to contacts saves it.

Save link only

Keep the link now, fill in the contact later.

  • Reads NFC tags (13.56 MHz). Not 125 kHz RFID or UHF.
  • Android reads continuously. iPhone reads while the Tap screen is open.
  • Nothing opens automatically. Payment and badge tags are ignored.
Clarify RFID versus NFC: phones read NFC tap cards, which is what most business tap cards use.
